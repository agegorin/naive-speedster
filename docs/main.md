# Naive Speedster

A minimalist racing game where players control a customizable car in a stark white open world environment.

## Core Concept

Drive through a small open world with various trials and challenges scattered throughout a clean, white aesthetic landscape. The focus is on exploration, speed, and building a connection with your vehicle through customization.

## Key Features

- **Crossplatform**: Designed for mobile, web, and desktop
- **Minimalist Environment**: White world with clean aesthetics
- **Open World Structure**: Small open world with trials/tracks positioned throughout
- **Car Customization**: Players can customize and modify their vehicle
- **Exploration-Based**: Discover and complete trials at your own pace

## Technology Stack

**Engine**: Godot 4

Selected for its excellent cross-platform support, lightweight nature, and rapid prototyping capabilities. The engine provides built-in 3D physics suitable for racing mechanics while being free and open source. See [[Engine Selection]] for full evaluation.

## Ideas

### Car Parts with Personalities
Each car part could have its own personality or characteristics:
- Engine that "prefers" certain speeds
- Wheels that have different "moods" affecting handling
- Parts that develop relationships with each other over time
- Performance influenced by how well parts "get along"
- Visual or audio feedback reflecting the parts' personalities

This could create an emergent gameplay where vehicle customization isn't just about stats, but about creating a harmonious (or chaotic) machine with character.
